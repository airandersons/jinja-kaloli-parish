Sacrament of Baptism

Baptism is the first sacrament of Christian initiation, freeing us from original sin and making us children of God. Through water and the Holy Spirit, we are born again into new life in Christ.

Requirements:

  • Parents must be registered parishioners
  • Attend pre-baptismal preparation class
  • Choose at least one godparent (practicing Catholic)
  • Provide birth certificate

Contact Parish Office to schedule: +256 123 456 789

Register for Baptism

First Holy Communion

Children typically receive their First Communion in second grade or above, after appropriate preparation. This sacrament deepens their relationship with Jesus, who gives Himself as spiritual food.

Requirements:

  • Child must be baptized
  • Complete one year of catechism preparation
  • Attend First Reconciliation before First Communion
  • Regular Mass attendance with family

Contact our Catechism Coordinator

Enroll in Preparation

Confirmation

Confirmation perfects our baptismal grace and seals us with the gift of the Holy Spirit. It strengthens us to be witnesses of Christ in the world.

Requirements:

  • Must be baptized and have received First Communion
  • Two-year preparation program (typically ages 14-16)
  • Choose a confirmation sponsor
  • Participate in a retreat

Contact Youth Ministry Office

Join Confirmation Class

Sacrament of Marriage

Christian marriage is a covenant through which a man and a woman establish between themselves a partnership of the whole of life, ordered toward the good of the spouses and the procreation of children.

Requirements:

  • Both parties must be free to marry (no prior bond)
  • Six months advance notice required
  • Complete marriage preparation course
  • Provide baptismal certificates (issued within 6 months)

Schedule an appointment with the priest

Begin Marriage Preparation

Sacrament of Reconciliation

Also known as Confession or Penance, this sacrament offers us God's merciful forgiveness for our sins and reconciles us with the Church.

How to Participate:

  • Regular confession times: Saturdays 4:00-5:30 PM
  • Examination of conscience before coming
  • First confession for children precedes First Communion
  • Individual appointments available

Call to schedule an appointment

View Confession Times

Anointing of the Sick

This sacrament provides spiritual and even physical strength to those who are seriously ill, suffering, or facing major surgery. It offers Christ's healing presence and comfort.

When to Request:

  • Before serious surgery or medical procedure
  • During serious illness or frailty due to age
  • For those nearing the end of life
  • Can be repeated if condition worsens

Call Parish Office anytime for emergencies
