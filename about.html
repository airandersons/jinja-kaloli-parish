About Our Parish

Discover our rich history, faith, and community

Welcome to

St Charles Lwanga Jinja Kaloli

Located in the heart of Kawempe – Kampala, Uganda, our parish stands as a beacon of faith, hope, and love since its establishment.

We are a vibrant Catholic community dedicated to building a Christ-centered environment where families grow in faith, young people discover their purpose, and everyone experiences the transforming love of God. Our parish is more than just a place of worship – it's a spiritual home where thousands of faithful gather to celebrate the sacraments, participate in ministries, and serve our neighbors in need.

Our Journey

Parish History

1974

The Beginning

St Charles Lwanga Jinja Kaloli Catholic Parish was established as a small chapel serving the local community. The first Mass was celebrated under a simple structure with just 50 families.

1985

Church Building

The current church building was constructed through the tireless efforts of parishioners. This marked a significant milestone in our growth.

2000

Expansion & Growth

With the growing community, we expanded our facilities including the parish hall, classrooms for catechism, and the priest's residence.

2024

Today

Now serving over 5,000 families, Mutungo Catholic Parish continues to grow in faith and service, embracing modern ways to spread the Gospel.

Our Mission

To build a Christ-centered community rooted in faith, love, and service, where every person encounters the living God and grows in holiness.

  • Proclaim the Gospel with joy
  • Celebrate the Sacraments worthily
  • Serve the poor and marginalized
  • Form disciples for Christ

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope and a model of authentic Christian community in Kampala and beyond.

  • A parish where families thrive
  • Youth passionate about faith
  • Active in social justice
  • Welcoming to all
