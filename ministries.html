Youth Ministry

Our Youth Ministry is a vibrant community of young people (ages 13-25) passionate about faith, friendship, and making a difference. We believe young people aren't just the future of the Church—they are the Church today.

Activities & Events:

  • Weekly youth nights with praise, worship, and talks
  • Annual retreats and camps
  • Community service projects
  • Sports tournaments and social events
  • Leadership training programs
Meeting Time: Fridays, 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM
Location: Parish Hall
Coordinator: Sarah Namutebi

Music Ministry

Four choirs serve at different Masses: Sunday 8am Choir, 10am Choir, Youth Choir, and Luganda Choir. All welcome—no experience required, just a joyful heart!

Practices: Wednesdays 5:00 PM

Catechism

Religious education for children (Pre-K through 7th grade) and adults preparing for sacraments. Classes run from September through May.

Classes: Sundays 9:00 AM

Catholic Women Association

Women gathering for prayer, fellowship, and service. Activities include visiting the sick, supporting families in need, and organizing parish events.

Meeting: 1st Saturday of month, 3:00 PM

Catholic Men Association

Men committed to spiritual growth, family life, and parish leadership. Activities include breakfast meetings, service projects, and fellowship.

Meeting: 2nd Sunday after 10am Mass

Altar Servers

Training: Saturdays 4:00 PM

How to Join a Ministry

Becoming part of a ministry is simple and welcoming

1

Choose a Ministry

Explore the ministries above and find one that matches your interests and gifts.

2

Contact the Coordinator

Reach out to the ministry coordinator listed or call the parish office for guidance.

3

Attend a Meeting

Come to a meeting or event. No pressure—just come and see if it's a good fit for you.

4

Get Involved

Join, participate, and discover the joy of serving and growing in community.
