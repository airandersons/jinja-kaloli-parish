Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a parishioner?

Visit the parish office during working hours with your baptismal certificate and identification. Our staff will guide you through the simple registration process.

How can I request a baptismal certificate?

Contact the parish office via phone or email. Certificates can be picked up in person or mailed to you for a small fee.

Do you offer marriage preparation courses?

Yes, we offer regular marriage preparation programs. Contact the parish office at least six months before your intended wedding date.

How do I arrange a sick visit or anointing?

Call the parish office anytime for emergencies. For planned visits, please give us at least 24 hours notice.
