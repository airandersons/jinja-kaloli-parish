Dear beloved parishioners and visitors, it is my joy to welcome you to our spiritual home. St Charles Lwanga Jinja Kaloli is more than just a church – it is a family where we walk together in faith, support one another in love, and serve our community with compassion. Whether you're joining us for the first time or you've been here for years, know that you are always welcome in God's house.

In Christ's service,
