Easter Vigil Celebration

March 30, 2024 8:00 PM

Join us for the most solemn and beautiful liturgy of the year as we celebrate the Resurrection of our Lord. The Easter Vigil includes the Service of Light, readings from salvation history, baptism of new members, and the first Eucharist of Easter.

Highlights:

  • Blessing of the New Fire
  • Seven Readings from Scripture
  • Baptism of Catechumens
  • Solemn Eucharistic Celebration

Rehearsal: March 29, 5:00 PM (for ministers)

Potluck Reception: Following Mass in Parish Hall
